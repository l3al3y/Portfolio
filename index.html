More about me ＋

My foundation is computer engineering. I like working across the boundaries: a camera and a model, a sensor and its firmware, an agent and the infrastructure that keeps it running.

Some projects start with a technical question. Others start with an everyday inconvenience—like wanting to read without touching the screen. Building them is how I understand the parts, test my assumptions, and find the next thing to improve.

I’m based in Klang Valley, open to relocation, and looking for opportunities to build practical technology alongside people I can learn from.
