# Biggest Missing Feature (This Is What Will Make Recruiters Remember You)

Right now your portfolio **tells** people you're skilled.

It doesn't fully **prove** it.

Add a dedicated **Case Study** page for each major project with:

* The problem you were solving
* Your design decisions
* Architecture diagrams
* Photos or screenshots
* Technologies used
* Challenges you overcame
* Measurable results
* GitHub repository
* Demo video (if available)
* What you learned

A recruiter spending just 5–10 minutes on those case studies will come away with a much stronger understanding of your engineering ability than from certificates or skill lists alone.
