Experience & Approach

Graduate Engineer in Computer Engineering (UTeM). I connect AI, hardware, and software to make intelligent systems practical outside of Jupyter notebooks.

Engineering Philosophy

An AI model in isolation is incomplete. Its real value begins when it is bound to physical constraints: camera frame rates, hardware cache alignments, sensor noise, power budgets, and unreliable network links.

My focus is on end-to-end integration: training lightweight computer vision architectures, writing low-latency C/C++ firmware, designing multi-agent consensus pipelines, and packaging high-availability Android release APKs for thousands of active users.
