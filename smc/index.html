01 / ON YOUR PHONE

Install.
Open. Test.

  1. Download and install the APK.

    Save SMC-Crows-resource-repair-v3.apk on your phone and open it with Android’s package installer. For the previous community build, choose Update to keep its data. You do not need to uninstall it.

  2. Open SMC on Wi-Fi.

    Existing complete resources are reused, and the 29-file repair is applied automatically. On a fresh installation, setup downloads and verifies 5.56 GB, then extracts the 119,076-resource store and applies the repair. Keep setup open; its progress screen and notification show the work. Interrupted downloads can resume.

  3. Let setup finish, then test the game.

    On a fresh install, open the original game once using the setup screen. Return to setup and explicitly confirm the first-start attempt before applying Crows. Then open SMC and test pilots, skins, the battle-pass screen and a match. Existing patched installs resume after the repair. No Termux, root or manual folder copying is needed.

If it fails, the original unmodified game and the original Crows PC kit remain the reference installation path.
