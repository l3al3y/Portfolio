2. Scope of This Policy

This policy explains how Hermes accesses, uses, stores, and protects information obtained through Google APIs, including Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Docs, and Google Contacts (the "Google Services").

By authorising Hermes to access your Google account, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

4. How We Use Google User Data

Google user data is used exclusively to provide the application's automation features to the account owner. Specifically:

  • Reading email to summarise, triage, and surface messages the owner cares about
  • Sending email that the owner has composed, requested, or approved
  • Reading calendar data to generate daily briefings and detect conflicts
  • Reading and writing documents, spreadsheets, and files the owner works with
  • Reading contacts to correctly address correspondence

We do NOT use Google user data to:

  • Sell, rent, trade, or transfer data to any third party
  • Serve advertising, or build advertising or profiling audiences
  • Train, fine-tune, or improve any machine-learning or AI model
  • Determine credit-worthiness, or for any lending or insurance purpose
  • Conduct surveillance, or for any purpose not described in this policy

Limited Use disclosure: Hermes' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Storage and Security

Local storage only. Hermes runs as a self-hosted application on the Operator's own personal devices (an Android phone and a personal laptop). There is no multi-tenant cloud service, no shared database, and no external server that stores Google user data.

Security measures in place:

  • OAuth 2.0 credentials are stored only in local, access-restricted credential files (Unix permission 600, owner-read-only)
  • OAuth client secrets and refresh tokens are never hardcoded into source code, and are never transmitted to any third party
  • All communication with Google APIs occurs over HTTPS/TLS
  • Access tokens are short-lived and refreshed automatically using the stored refresh token
  • No employee, contractor, or other individual besides the Operator has access to the data

Data retrieved from Google is processed locally and retained only as long as it is useful for the automation task at hand. Derived summaries and cached state may be stored locally on the Operator's devices.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, or disclose Google user data to any third party, except:

  • With your direction — if you explicitly ask the application to send or publish information
  • For legal reasons — if required to comply with a valid legal process, in which case the Operator will notify the account owner unless legally prohibited

No third-party analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or data brokers are integrated with this application.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally cached data may be deleted at any time by the account owner, and is automatically pruned when it is no longer needed. To have all data removed:

  • Revoke access: visit myaccount.google.com/permissions, select this application, and choose Remove access. This immediately invalidates all stored tokens, and Hermes can no longer access any Google data.
  • Request deletion: email cabalme4@gmail.com to request deletion of any locally retained copies of your data, which will be actioned within 30 days.
